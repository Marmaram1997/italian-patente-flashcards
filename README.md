# Patente B — Flashcard Italiano → Persiano

1,949 flashcards built from the Italian driving-licence (Patente B) handout: Italian on the front, Persian meaning on the back.

**Use it:** open the GitHub Pages link on your phone and choose *Add to Home Screen* (works offline after the first visit).

Features: tap to flip, swipe to move, filter by chapter, search in Italian or Persian, shuffle, "I know it" tracking (saved on your device), list view.

Files: `index.html` (app), `cards.json` (all words), `manifest.webmanifest` + `sw.js` (installable / offline), icons.
